# Privacy Policy — Supernova Emoji

**Last updated:** 2026-08-07

Supernova Emoji is designed for privacy.

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

The app and keyboard:

- do not create accounts;
- do not use analytics or tracking;
- do not show ads;
- do not require network access;
- do not require Full Access;
- do not read or store what you type;
- do not access contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.

## What is stored on device

Only local product preferences, for example:

- selected theme;
- selected category;
- favorite emoji identifiers;
- a short, bounded list of recent emoji identifiers.

These values stay on the device (and optionally in an App Group container if enabled later). They are never uploaded.

## Keyboard Full Access

Supernova Emoji is configured with **Requests Open Access = NO**.

You should leave **Allow Full Access** off when enabling the keyboard.

## Children

The product is intended to be suitable for family use. No personal information about children is collected.

## Contact

For privacy questions about this open product repository, open a GitHub issue on the project repository. Do not include personal or child-identifying information in issues.
